ThreadHaus PostgreSQL streaming replication - insert on primary, read from replica
Collected: 2026-11-10 02:25:45Z

Commands (psql over the container-local Unix socket, no passwords involved):
  docker exec threadhaus-pg-primary psql -U postgres -d threadhaus -c "INSERT INTO customer ... RETURNING customer_id, full_name, email, phone;"
  poll: docker exec threadhaus-pg-replica psql -U postgres -d threadhaus -Atc "SELECT count(*) FROM customer WHERE email = 'adaeze.okafor@example.com.ng';"
  docker exec threadhaus-pg-replica psql -U postgres -d threadhaus -c "SELECT customer_id, full_name, email, phone, created_at FROM customer WHERE email = 'adaeze.okafor@example.com.ng';"
